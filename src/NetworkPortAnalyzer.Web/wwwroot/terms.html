1. Authorized use

Use JackPeek only on devices and networks you own or have permission to investigate. Follow applicable law and your organization's security, monitoring, and retention policies. Passive capture still requires appropriate authorization to observe and retain network information.

The internet speed test generates standard HTTPS test traffic to an external test provider after sign-in. Use it only where that traffic is permitted by local policy.

You are responsible for the workstation, browser, capture driver, storage paths, and people who can access them. Do not expose the local interface through a proxy or tunnel to an untrusted network. If you do not agree to these conditions, stop using the application.

2. Interpreting results

JackPeek listens for advertised LLDP and CDP information. It does not verify switch ownership or identity, authenticate advertisements, validate every network configuration, or certify a cable, switch port, or network.

A missing result does not establish that a device is absent or a port is faulty. A missing field means no value was available from the decoded advertisement. Devices can provide incomplete, outdated, conflicting, or inaccurate information. Confirm findings through your approved operational processes before making changes.

A checksum comparison checks whether a record matches its stored checksum. It is not a digital signature, proof of authorship, or an independent guarantee that a record has never been altered.

3. Evidence responsibility

You control capture activity, report storage, export, and sharing. Reports may contain sensitive network information, workstation details, and personal identifiers. Review the Privacy Policy and configure identity, encryption, mirroring, and retention to suit your requirements.

Maintain any backups you need. Archive mirroring depends on storage availability and can fail independently of local saving. Local deletion does not remove copies or exports. Exported reports are readable even when the stored source record was encrypted.
